# Trademark check: "Fast Forward Typing" (first screening, 2026-09-24)

Method: TMview (the joint search of EUIPO, French INPI and German DPMA), fuzzy search for
"fast forward typing", "fast forward" and "fastforward", about 870 marks reviewed, then filtered to
live marks (registered or filed) in the classes that matter for us: 9 (software), 41 (education,
training), 42 (online software services), 35 (business services). Not checked: UK, US, other
countries, unregistered rights. This is a screening, not a legal clearance.

## Result in one line

No mark named "Fast Forward Typing" or anything with "typing" exists in the three registers.
The exact words "Fast Forward" are registered by several other companies, and four of them touch
our field closely enough that a trademark attorney should look before you invest in the brand.

## What it means

- Using a name is not registering it. Owners of a similar earlier mark can send a cease-and-desist
  letter or oppose your later application if customers could confuse the two. "Fast Forward" is a
  common phrase (thousands of hits), so each owner's protection is narrow, but the four above
  cover education and software, which is exactly our field.
- "Typing" is descriptive for a typing course and adds little protection. The distinctive part of
  our name is "Fast Forward", which is also what the others share.
- Our extra sign ">>" and the logo help a little, not enough to rely on.

## Recommendation

1. Before spending money on brand building (logo, ads, print), pay a trademark attorney for a
   proper similarity opinion on the four marks above (usually a fixed fee, a few hundred euros).
2. Do not file "Fast Forward" alone as your own mark; file only after the opinion.
3. Keep the rebrand option alive (the "Tactus" domain research is in the business-ideas repo).
   A clean, distinctive name is cheaper now than after launch.
4. Repeat this search once a year and before any filing. Use the official sites to confirm:
   euipo.europa.eu (eSearch plus), bases-marques.inpi.fr, register.dpma.de.
